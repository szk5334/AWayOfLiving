Maybe a religion hurt you, and it left a hole shaped like the thing it was supposed to be.

Maybe you still want meaning that comes from somewhere larger than yourself, but you don't want to kneel to get it. You want the stories, the rhythm, the sense of belonging to something, without the fear.

*A Way of Living* is a short book that offers exactly that: a creation story, seven ways to live, and a practice that needs only a notebook and a pen. It has a god at the crossroads who asks for nothing.

No worship. No guilt. No one keeping a ledger. Only a way to live, if you want it.

## Do you see yourself?

You've lived several lives inside this one, with more crafts, careers, and obsessions than anyone can keep track of. You've been called scattered, restless, too much.

You've never quite belonged to the room you were born in, but you're oddly at home in doorways and hallways, at crossroads and in-between places. You're the one people call to translate, the friend who understands both sides.

You notice luck. You make things out of whatever's at hand. You can't stop asking questions.

You know the shadow side too: the half-finished projects, the plans that grow too large, the clever way you can talk yourself out of what you already know.

None of this makes you broken. A life is made of crossings, and you were built for them. That's what it means to be **Mercurian**, and this book was written for you.

## What's inside

### ☿ A god who walks beside you

Mercury, Hermes to the Greeks, invented the lyre on the morning he was born and stole his brother's cattle by evening. He is the god of roads and thresholds, of travelers, luck, and language. He's the guide who walks souls across the last crossing and never asks whether they deserve to arrive. He doesn't want your worship. He's simply glad of the company.

### The One Soul

You die, and there's no gate, no scale, no judge. There's a crossroads at dusk and a young man with winged sandals, who tells you that there is only one soul, and everyone is you on another road. The world is an egg, and something is growing inside it.

If every kindness lands on yourself a few lives from now, there's no one to earn anything from. There's only you, meeting yourself over and over, becoming a little more whole.

### The Seven Wanderers

The seven classical planets aren't commandments. They're capacities a whole soul grows into, one for each day of the week, each with its own kind of joy:

| | Wanderer | Way | Teaching |
|:-:|---|---|---|
| ☉ | Sun · Sunday | Living Radiantly | *Your light takes nothing from anyone else's.* |
| ☽ | Moon · Monday | Living in Season | *No state is final simply because you're inside it.* |
| ♂ | Mars · Tuesday | Living Boldly | *Guard what you love, and count yourself among it.* |
| ☿ | Mercury · Wednesday | Living Mercurially | *There is almost always another road.* |
| ♃ | Jupiter · Thursday | Living Jovially | *Reality is responsive, but not obedient.* |
| ♀ | Venus · Friday | Living Sumptuously | *Sweetness isn't a reward; it's the point.* |
| ♄ | Saturn · Saturday | Living Deliberately | *The limit gives the thing its shape.* |

### Gifts, not sins

What the old lists warned you about turns out to be one of two things: a gift you were wrongly taught to fear, or a Wanderer leaning too far one way. Pleasure belongs to Venus. Anger is information from Mars. Rest belongs to Saturn. Doubt is Mercury holding beliefs loosely enough for the world to correct them.

None of these need forgiveness. They only need room. When something leans too far, another Wanderer offers the way back. There's no penance and no confession, only rebalancing.

### The practice: a notebook and a pen

Every visit is a small threshold:

> **Hello. Remember. Empty. Reread. Continue, or don't. Carry. Goodbye.**

You write your life down and come back to read it. You answer what past-you wrote, so the practice is one life speaking to itself across time. You mark open questions with a **Q** and leave them open as long as they need. You mark small promises with a **C**, and every one you keep makes your word a little heavier. An unkept one isn't a failure. It's a map of what you actually value.

The more of your life you share with the notebook, the more its **Genius** grows: a companionable spirit, something like a housecat of the mind.

There are no streaks and no days to make up. When you wander off, and you will, coming back needs no apology. Just say hello.

### Magick, if you want it

An optional fourth part offers workings for those who want them: Raziel, keeper of secrets and translator; AONT, the Enochian Spirit of Air; emotional transmutation, declarations, and sigils. Every working ends with a step you actually take. And the book will never tell you a working failed because you didn't believe hard enough.

## What this is not

This is not a religion. There is no worship here, no supplication, and nothing demanded of you. You never owe an apology for doubting, wandering, failing, changing your mind, or coming back.

You don't have to leave anything behind to take this up. Mercury has always been comfortable at crossroads, where many roads meet. If you set it down, nothing follows you out the door.
